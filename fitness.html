Fitness: Balanced

Complex underneath.
Simple on top.

The app is designed to answer a few practical questions: What should I do today? Am I improving? What can I become next? What does the app actually know about me?

Goals, current ability, available time, equipment, readiness, and reviewed movement progressions are combined into a training path that can adapt without erasing your history.

Release information

Privacy and support.

Does Fitness: Balanced diagnose injuries or pain?

No. Pain or problem feedback can change, regress, substitute, or stop training. The app does not diagnose the cause.

Does browsing exercises create progress?

No. Progress and achievements are evidence-based. Looking at exercises or completing app actions does not manufacture a fitness level.
