Privacy

Fitness: Balanced
Privacy Policy.

Last updated October 2026. This policy describes the current local-first iPhone release and must change if the product's data flows change.

Current release model: Fitness: Balanced stores its fitness state locally on the device. The current release does not implement a Fitness: Balanced account backend or cross-device fitness-data synchronization.

Information the app can store

Depending on what you choose to provide and use, Fitness: Balanced can store the following information on your device:

  • optional age, height, and weight;
  • goals, goal priority, planning horizon, schedule, available days, equipment, and training experience;
  • optional sport and discipline priorities;
  • assessment answers and estimated physical capabilities;
  • archetype and presentation preferences;
  • workout history and performance evidence;
  • daily readiness responses;
  • achievements and current/historical capability state;
  • temporary movement restrictions created from pain or stop feedback; and
  • an unfinished-workout draft used to restore an interrupted session.

Storage and transmission

The current Fitness: Balanced release is local-first. Fitness data is stored in the app's local device storage. Before public App Store submission, the exact production binary and dependencies are reviewed again for any platform, crash-reporting, analytics, advertising, cloud, or other third-party data behavior.

Health and medical information

Fitness: Balanced provides fitness and training guidance. It does not diagnose injuries, pain, disease, or medical conditions. Pain/problem feedback can change or stop training and can temporarily restrict a movement pattern.

Your control

The app includes an Erase local fitness data control. It removes Fitness: Balanced onboarding, assessments, workout history, capability state, achievements, readiness, preferences, and unfinished workout data stored by the app on that device.

Children

The current product is not designed or marketed as a child-directed service. App Store age-rating and audience configuration are completed as part of release review.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when product data flows change, including if a later release adds account sync, analytics, advertising, Apple Health integration, cloud storage, or other connected services.
