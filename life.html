Life: Balanced

Less switching.
More living.

Life gets scattered across lists, messages, calendars, and notes. Life: Balanced is being built to give you a clearer view of today and help you plan tomorrow.

It's designed for individuals first, with household coordination and shared workflows that respect everyone's permissions.

This page describes work in development, not a claim that all features are publicly available.

Questions

Good things to know.

Is Life: Balanced available to download?

Life: Balanced is under active development and private testing. A general public launch has not been announced on this site.

Will it work with the other Balanced products?

The products are being designed around shared identity and interoperable services, with separate data access and permissions for each product.

What about financial features?

Financial planning and bank connectivity are part of the broader Life: Balanced roadmap, but they are being developed and validated separately from everyday planning features.

Can organizations use Balanced too?

Business: Balanced is a separate product in development for organizational operations, finance, projects, and more.
