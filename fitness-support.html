Support.

Quick answers for the current Fitness: Balanced iPhone release.

How is today's workout chosen?

The planner combines active goals, assessment and workout evidence, available time, confirmed equipment, readiness, and reviewed movement progressions. Unknown capabilities remain visibly unassessed instead of receiving invented scores.

What if an exercise hurts?

Use the Pain / problem control in the workout. Fitness: Balanced can change or stop that movement and can temporarily restrict the affected movement pattern. It does not diagnose the cause of pain.

What if the app closes during a workout?

The unfinished session is stored locally. On relaunch you can resume from the saved exercise and set, or discard only that unfinished workout.

How do I erase my data?

Open You → About & privacy → Erase local fitness data. The app asks for confirmation before removing its local fitness state.

How do I understand a score or goal percentage?

Open the relevant fitness domain or goal from Progress. Fitness: Balanced shows evidence coverage and leaves unknown signals unknown instead of filling missing data with a neutral value.

Beta feedback

During TestFlight testing, use Apple's Send Beta Feedback option from the TestFlight app or the installed beta's feedback workflow. A permanent public support contact will be added before general App Store release.
